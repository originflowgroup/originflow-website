Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you contact us through this website, by email, or on WhatsApp, we receive your name, contact details, and any message you send us. We do not collect this information through any other means unless you provide it directly.

How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about Originflow Group's services. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Contact form

Messages sent through the contact form on this website are delivered to us by Web3Forms, a third-party form service, which processes your name, email address, and message in order to forward them to our email inbox. Web3Forms handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Our services

Some of our services, including AI for Aging and the Vernacular Voice-AI Agency, involve phone calls and messages. Before we begin any service for you, we will explain what is recorded, how it is used, and who can see it, and we will ask for your consent.

Demo content

The demo video on this website uses voices and messages belonging to the Originflow Group team and our own family members, shared with their permission. If you message our WhatsApp demo number, we receive the messages you send and use them only to run the demo and respond to you. WhatsApp messages are also processed by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

Analytics and cookies

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand how many people visit this site and which pages they view, in aggregate. We also store your cookie-consent choice in your browser's local storage so we do not ask you again on every visit.

Data storage

Information you send us is used only to get back to you and is not kept longer than needed to respond to your inquiry.

Your rights

You can request that we delete any information you've shared with us at any time by emailing us directly.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, reach out at originflowgroup@gmail.com.
